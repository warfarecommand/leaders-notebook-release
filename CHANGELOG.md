# Release notes

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-12)

- Today, Marines, Calendar, and Sessions track counseling dates, relationships, targets, and records.
- Three editable record formats support full counseling, NCOs, and junior Marines, with review, acknowledgment, amendments, and Word output.
- Voluntary mentorship stays private and separate from counseling status and exports.
- Roster and program handoffs exclude records and notes by default; the GenAI.mil method packet never includes records, notes, or mentoring.
- Setup verifies the release and opens one notebook; pasted updates preserve the same address and personal data.
- Read-only shares show status and the program; 0.1.0 has no telemetry, registration, or usage counts.
